# 7–9 Minute Demonstration

1. **Problem — 45 seconds:** explain one shared cash reserve and non-interchangeable provider e-money.
2. **Dashboard — 60 seconds:** sign in as Operations; show provider separation, freshness, nearest pressure, and queue.
3. **Scenario A — 75 seconds:** sign out, sign in as Admin, load A, open Rahman Store, and show Provider A pressure and confidence.
4. **Scenario B — 90 seconds:** load B; distinguish shared-cash pressure from unusual repeated activity. Emphasize that the pattern is not proof of fraud.
5. **Scenario C — 60 seconds:** show missing/conflicting feeds, confidence deductions, and the suppressed precise forecast.
6. **Scenario D — 90 seconds:** as Operations, claim, acknowledge, note, and escalate. Sign out; sign in as Risk; start progress, resolve, and show the timeline.
7. **Optional AI — 30 seconds:** if deliberately enabled, show one structured summary or translation, its uncertainty/source label, and the human-review boundary. If offline, show that controls are hidden and continue.
8. **Evidence — 45 seconds:** show exact-set validation metrics, deterministic seed, architecture, tests, privacy, and limitations.
9. **Close — 30 seconds:** connect early liquidity insight, explainable review, and human-owned coordination.

## Manual rehearsal checklist

- Start from a clean Compose volume and confirm all services become healthy.
- Complete the sequence without database edits or post-build internet access.
- Confirm role changes use sign-out/sign-in; no role-switch UI or API exists.
- Keep provider e-money and shared cash visibly separate at every step.
- Confirm Bengali text, keyboard focus, 1366×768 layout, and the 768px fallback.
